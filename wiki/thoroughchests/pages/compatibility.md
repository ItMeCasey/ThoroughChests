## Recipe viewers

Every crafting recipe shows in the viewer crafting category. Two categories of our own: **Chest Upgrade** (a chest and a card give the next chest) and **Augment Upgrade** (the installed level plus the next item give the next level).

## Info overlays

SMWTI and Jade show the level, the slots used, the installed augment, the owner of a locked chest and, in SMWTI, the three most common items.
