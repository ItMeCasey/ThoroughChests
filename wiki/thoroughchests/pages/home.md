Thorough Chests adds one chest block in five levels. Each chest is a single block with its own big inventory, the vanilla lid animation and the vanilla sounds. The screen scrolls and searches, and one augment per chest makes it do work for you.

## Start here

- [[install]] for versions and the files you need.
- [[recipes]] for what you craft.
- [[chests]] for the levels and the upgrade cards.
- [[screen]] for scrolling and search.
- [[augments]] for the six augments and their levels.
- [[scripting]] for ModJS and KubeJS.

> [!NOTE]
> There are no double chests. A bigger chest is a higher level, not two blocks side by side.
