title: Recipes
summary: Every crafting recipe of the mod.
---
All recipes are datapack JSON under `data/thoroughchests/recipe`, so datapacks and KubeJS can change them.

## Chests and cards

| Result | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Copper Chest | A vanilla chest in the middle, eight copper ingots around it |
| Iron, Gold, Diamond or Netherite Upgrade Card | Four ingots (diamonds for the diamond card) in a plus shape around one paper |
| Iron, Gold, Diamond and Netherite Chest | No recipe: use the cards on a chest one level down |

## Augments

Shape `ABA / BCB / ABA` with redstone for B.

| Augment | A, level 1 / 2 / 3 | C (the heart) |
|---|---|---|
| Compact (one level) | iron ingot | crafting table |
| Furnace | iron ingot / gold ingot / diamond | furnace |
| Blast Furnace | iron ingot / gold ingot / diamond | blast furnace |
| Magnet | iron ingot / gold ingot / diamond | ender pearl |
| Feeder | iron ingot / gold ingot / diamond | hopper |
| Void (one level) | iron ingot | obsidian |

Level 2 and level 3 augments are complete items of their own. They are applied on top of the installed lower level; see [[augments]].
